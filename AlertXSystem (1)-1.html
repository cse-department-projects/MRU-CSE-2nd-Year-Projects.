Live Coverage Map

Every station, one composite view.

Each marker represents a live sensor cluster. Pulsing markers indicate an active or recent event above monitoring threshold — sized by magnitude, colored by alert tier.

Classification

A four-tier system, escalated automatically.

Magnitude, depth, population density, and historical fault behavior are weighed together the moment a signal crosses threshold.

TIER 01

Monitoring

Minor tremor logged. No public alert. Visible only on the operator dashboard.

TIER 02

Advisory

Moderate activity. Push notification sent to registered users in the affected district.

TIER 03

Warning

Significant shaking likely. SMS + app + siren network triggered across the region.

TIER 04

Emergency

Severe event. All channels triggered, evacuation guidance issued, authorities notified.

Pipeline

From ground motion to a phone in someone's pocket.

Five stages run in sequence, most of them in well under a second.

01

Detect

Seismic sensors across the station network sample ground motion continuously and flag any reading above the local noise floor.

~0.3s
02

Analyze

Waveform data is cross-checked against neighboring stations to estimate magnitude, depth, and epicenter, filtering out false triggers.

~1.2s
03

Classify

The event is scored against population density and historical fault data, and assigned one of four alert tiers.

~0.8s
04

Alert

Notifications route through the channels appropriate to the tier — in-app push, SMS, or public siren — in the region's local language.

~2.1s
05

Respond

Affected users receive nearest-shelter guidance and evacuation routes; authorities get a live dashboard of the unfolding event.
