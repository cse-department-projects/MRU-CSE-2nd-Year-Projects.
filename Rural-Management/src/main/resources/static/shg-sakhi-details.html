School Uniform Stitching Unit

Sakhi SHG operates 6 industrial sewing machines in the Ward 1 community center. Contracted by the district education office to supply stitched uniforms for 4 primary schools in Rampur and neighboring villages (650+ uniforms stitched annually).

Annual Revenue: ₹1.95 Lakh 6 Direct Tailors

Organic Pickles & Masala Processing

Members procure fresh mangoes, lemons, and green chilies directly from Rampur farmers to prepare traditional home-style pickles with no artificial preservatives. Packaged in sanitized jars and distributed through local grocery shops and weekly mandi haats.

Monthly Sales: 180 Jars FSSAI Certified
