Eco-Friendly Jute Bag Manufacturing

Ujjwala SHG spearheads the plastic-free village drive by producing durable jute shopping bags, grocery totes, and cloth office document files used by the Gram Panchayat, schools, and local vendors.

Supplies to 14 Local Stores 100% Biodegradable

Handcrafted Kantha & Zari Embroidery

Skilled in fine hand needlework, members design ethnic dupattas, stoles, table runners, and festive wall hangings. Exhibited regularly at district craft melas, state Saras fairs, and government handicraft portals.
