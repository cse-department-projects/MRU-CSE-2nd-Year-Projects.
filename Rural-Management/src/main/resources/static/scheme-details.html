PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare · Direct Benefit Transfer
Active & Enrolling

Provides 100% centrally sponsored income support of ₹6,000 per annum to all eligible landholder farmer families, transferred directly into their Aadhaar-linked bank accounts in three equal 4-monthly tranches of ₹2,000.

Financial Benefit₹6,000 / Year (DBT)
Eligibility CriteriaLandholding Farmer Families
Disbursement ModeAadhaar-seeded Bank A/C
Panchayat HelpdeskDesk 1 (Gram Sevak)
Required Documents for Rampur Desk:
Aadhaar Card Land Record (Khatauni / Jamabandi) Bank Passbook with IFSC Active Mobile No. Linked to Aadhaar
Official Portal Toll-Free Helpline: 155261 / 1800115526

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Comprehensive Yield & Weather Risk Crop Insurance
Active For Rabi Season

Insures farmers against non-preventable natural risks (drought, dry spells, floods, inundation, pests, diseases, hailstorms, and unseasonal rains). Farmers pay nominal premium (1.5% for Rabi, 2% for Kharif) with 85%+ subsidized by Government.

Farmer Premium Share1.5% (Rabi) / 2.0% (Kharif)
Coverage TypeSowing to Post-Harvest
Claim AssessmentSatellite & Drone CCE
Intimation WindowWithin 72 Hours of Loss
Required Documents:
Sowing Certificate / Self-Declaration Land Revenue Record / Khasra No. Bank Account Details Aadhaar Card
Official Portal Crop Loss Toll-Free: 1800 180 1551

Soil Health Card Scheme

Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare · Scientific Soil Nutrition
100% Free Testing

Provides customized soil assessment cards every two years, analyzing 12 critical chemical and physical parameters (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, Bo, pH, EC, OC) to advise farmers on exact fertilizer doses, saving up to 25% on input costs.

Cost to Farmer100% Free of Cost
Parameters Tested12 Macro & Micro Nutrients
Validity2 Years (Cycle II)
Soil Collection CenterRampur Village Office
How to Submit Soil Sample:

Collect 'V' shaped soil slices from 8-10 points in your plot (depth 15cm). Mix thoroughly, pack 500g in clean bag with survey number and drop at Rampur Panchayat desk.

Official Portal Next Collection Drive: Monday 10:00 AM

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

NABARD & Commercial Banks · Flexible Low-Interest Farm Credit
Fast-Track Approval

Single-window institutional credit facility enabling farmers to purchase seeds, fertilizers, tractor diesel, crop labor, and meet post-harvest storage expenses at a heavily subsidized effective interest rate of 4% per year on prompt repayment.

Collateral-Free LimitUp to ₹3,00,000
Effective Interest Rate4% p.a. (with subvention)
Tenure / Validity5 Years (Revolving)
Participating BankSBI & Bank of Baroda
Eligibility & Process:

Open to all owner cultivators, tenant farmers, sharecroppers, and SHG joint liability groups. Submit land cultivation slip and Aadhaar to SBI Rampur branch for 14-day sanction.

NABARD KCC Portal Rampur Nodal Officer: +91 98765 44000

PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (Per Drop More Crop)

Ministry of Jal Shakti · Drip & Sprinkler Micro Irrigation Subsidy
55% Direct Subsidy

Encourages water-saving micro-irrigation systems to improve crop water productivity. Small and marginal farmers receive 55% direct financial subsidy on certified drip or sprinkler irrigation equipment installations.

Small/Marginal Subsidy55% of Approved Cost
Other Farmers Subsidy45% of Cost
Water Saving Rate40% to 50% Water Saved
Yield Enhancement20% to 35% Yield Increase
Application Steps:

Verify water source at Panchayat -> Select empanelled drip manufacturer -> Receive field survey & estimation -> Department sanction -> Installation with GPS tag.

Official Portal Empanelled Vendors List at Panchayat Office
