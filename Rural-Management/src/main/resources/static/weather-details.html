Field Operations Advisory For Current Weather

Irrigation Guidance

With soil moisture at 42% and 20% rain probability, shallow morning irrigation (2-3 cm) is recommended for early sown wheat (CRI stage) and mustard. Drip lines for onion can be operated on alternate mornings for 45 minutes.

StatusOptimal Irrigation Window: 06:30 AM – 09:30 AM

Sowing & Nursery Care

Average temperatures between 18°C and 28°C provide ideal seed germination conditions for late-sown wheat (variety DBW-187) and winter vegetables. Treat seeds with Trichoderma or Carboxin before broadcasting.

Soil ConditionFavorable Tillage & Seed Bed Moisture

Spraying & Pest Precaution

Winds are mild at 14 km/h with no gust alerts. Safe for herbicide and micronutrient foliar sprays. Avoid spraying after 11:30 AM when solar radiation spikes. Ensure spray nozzle is adjusted close to foliage.

Spray WindowSafe Spray Conditions (07:00 AM – 11:00 AM)

Storage & Threshing Advisory

Harvested paddy and sugarcane bundles should be stacked under elevated tarpaulin sheets in open threshing floors to avoid bottom dampness from morning dew. Maintain grain moisture below 12% before bagging.

Dew PrecautionKeep Stored Produce Dry & Ventilated
