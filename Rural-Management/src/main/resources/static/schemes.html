Government Schemes

Eligibility, benefits and how to apply

Active

PM-KISAN

Income support of ₹6,000/year to land-holding farmer families in three instalments.

Eligibility: Land-holding farmers
Benefits: ₹2,000 every 4 months

Active

PM Fasal Bima Yojana

Crop insurance against natural calamities, pests and diseases.

Eligibility: Farmers growing notified crops
Benefits: Compensation for crop loss

Active

Soil Health Card

Free soil testing and fertiliser recommendations.

Eligibility: Any farmer
Benefits: Printed nutrient status card

Active

Kisan Credit Card

Short-term credit for agriculture at low interest.

Eligibility: Farmers, tenant farmers, agri SHGs
Benefits: Flexible credit + interest subvention

Ongoing

MGNREGA

Guaranteed 100 days of wage employment for rural households.

Eligibility: Adult rural household members
Benefits: Wage employment + job card

Active

Ayushman Bharat

Health insurance for secondary and tertiary care.

Eligibility: As per SECC / state list
Benefits: Up to ₹5 lakh cover per family
