Village Dairy & Milk Chilling Center

Pragati SHG operates the central milk collection booth equipped with electronic fat-testing analyzers and digital weigh scales. Collecting 450 liters of fresh buffalo and cow milk twice daily from 68 cattle-rearing households.

Direct Bank Transfer to Milk Pourers Zero Middlemen

Organic Vermicompost Production Units

Utilizing cow dung and farm biomass, members maintain 8 shaded vermicompost beds using Australian Eisenia fetida earthworms. Producing premium organic manure packaged in 25kg bags for Rampur wheat and onion growers.

₹8 per Kg Selling Rate 100% Organic Manure
