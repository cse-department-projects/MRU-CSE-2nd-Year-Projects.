Wheat (गेंहू)

Varieties: HD-2967, DBW-187 · High-yielding
Rabi Season

Primary staple cereal crop of Rampur. High germination rate observed with zero-tillage sowing practices.

Village Acreage125 Hectares
Expected Yield22 Qtl / Acre
Govt MSP Rate₹2,275 / Qtl
Water Need4–5 Irrigations
Fertilizer DosageNPK 120:60:40 + Zn
Harvest WindowMarch – April
Pest & Disease Advisory:

Inspect crown root initiation for Yellow Rust. Spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1ml/liter if rust pustules appear on flag leaves.

Onion (प्याज)

Varieties: Nashik Red, Bhima Super
Rabi / Kharif

High-value cash vegetable crop concentrated along Ward 1 and Ward 2 with micro-irrigation lines.

Village Acreage54 Hectares
Expected Yield110 Qtl / Acre
Average Market Rate₹1,850 / Qtl
Water NeedLight Frequent Drip
Fertilizer DosageNPK 100:50:50 + S
Harvest WindowApril – May
Pest & Disease Advisory:

Thrips attacks during dry sunny spells. Apply Fipronil 5% SC or organic neem spray (10,000 ppm) with sticker solution.

Mustard (सरसों)

Varieties: Pusa Bold, Giriraj · High Oil Content
Rabi Season

Key oilseed crop requiring minimal water, ideal for well-drained loamy soils across central village blocks.

Village Acreage41 Hectares
Expected Yield18 Qtl / Acre
Govt MSP Rate₹5,650 / Qtl
Water Need2 Irrigations
Fertilizer DosageNPK 80:40:20 + Gypsum
Harvest WindowFebruary – March
Pest & Disease Advisory:

Mustard aphid threat during cloudy humid days. Set up yellow sticky traps and spray Dimethoate 30 EC if threshold exceeds 20 aphids/plant.

Rice / Paddy (धान)

Varieties: Swarna Sub-1, Basmati Hybrid
Kharif Season

Monsoon season staple grown on low-lying clay tracts with assured canal water in West and South Rampur.

Village Acreage98 Hectares
Expected Yield28 Qtl / Acre
Govt MSP Rate₹2,183 / Qtl
Water NeedShallow Standing Water
Fertilizer DosageNPK 120:60:40 + Zinc
Harvest WindowOctober – November
Pest & Disease Advisory:

Watch for Yellow Stem Borer and Brown Planthopper. Maintain alternate wetting and drying; apply Cartap Hydrochloride 4G granules.

Sugarcane (गन्ना)

Varieties: Co-0238, Co-86032
Annual Crop

High biomass commercial crop directly bound with contract procurement at the Rampur Cooperative Sugar Mill.

Village Acreage88 Hectares
Expected Yield380 Qtl / Acre
Fair Remunerative Price₹315 / Qtl
Water NeedHigh Regular Furrow
Fertilizer DosageNPK 250:100:100
Harvest WindowDecember – February
Pest & Disease Advisory:

Red Rot alert on aged ratoon fields. Use certified seed canes treated with Trichoderma viride before planting.

Cotton (कपास)

Bt Cotton Hybrid Varieties
Kharif Season

Leading fiber crop planted in deep black soils with integrated pest management (IPM) adherence in Ward 4.

Village Acreage72 Hectares
Expected Yield14 Qtl / Acre
Govt MSP Rate₹6,620 / Qtl
Water NeedMedium Controlled Drip
Fertilizer DosageNPK 100:50:50 + B
Harvest WindowNovember – January
Pest & Disease Advisory:

Mandatory pheromone traps for Pink Bollworm. Spray Neem extract during early vegetative stage; avoid unauthorized pesticides.
