Cold-Pressed Spices Grinding

Nari Shakti processes farm-fresh raw turmeric rhizomes, dried red chilies, and coriander seeds grown locally without chemical pesticides. Using low-RPM stainless steel pulverizers that preserve aroma, essential oils, and nutritional potency.

Pure Unadulterated Spices Lab Tested Purity

Stone-Ground Multigrain Flour & Daliya

To promote nutrition in rural households, members blend whole wheat, roasted gram, barley, and millet into fiber-rich stone-ground flour and cracked wheat daliya, vacuum-sealed in hygienic 1kg and 5kg packs.

High Dietary Fiber Direct to Anganwadi & Homes
